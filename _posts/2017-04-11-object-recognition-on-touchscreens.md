## Industrial project
Reaching the final year at TELECOM Nancy is a big step to the diploma, but we're still not completely prepared to professionnal life. The main topic of this year is based on the realization of a project offered by a real company called industrial project.<br />
The aim of this project is to make students aware of concrete problems faced by companies. In small team, we have to design a solution, from the specifications to the delivery phase, by esteeming the needs of the assigned company. 

## Context inside the startup
We were 3 students to work on the Object Recognition Project offered by a startup created a few years ago. This young society focused, until now, on digital marketing wich consist of selling large touch monitors along with softwares.

This kind of device are widespreading around us : in fast foods, museum, show rooms... In fact, from a commercial point of view, the user interaction they offer is a great asset.
If you're familiar with the startup's world, the key is to keep being innovative on the market. If you have a good idea, you'll quickly have competitors and it means nothing good. So if you want to be the leader on a market (and you WANT to), you constantly have to find new idea, new concept, new product.

Like a lot of other startup, this one uses it ressources at 100%. It also means that it has a lack of means, financialy and humanly. Good for them, the industrial project at TELECOM Nancy is a way to get 3 young and motivated (nearly) engineers at low price. Good for us, it also means that what we'll do will really matter. It's a true need and the startup put its trust in us. This is not this kind of boring projects in big companies that will never been used. We'are asked to find a solution to a problem the company doesn't know how to solve. 

It consist of a new way of interaction with end users : offering them the possibility to use object on touchscreens to interact with applications.

## The project
As I said it earlier, this is something new. Our startup doesn't have a clue on how to do that and she makes an important bet by trusting us to find a solution in less than half a year.
